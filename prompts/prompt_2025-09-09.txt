Background for 2d side-scrolling game, which have 4 separate horizontal layers. Layer 1: The far background features a gradient sky with subtle color transitions, dotted with distant clouds and faint mountain silhouettes to create depth. Layer 2: The mid-distant layer includes rolling hills and sparse trees, rendered with muted colors to emphasize distance. Layer 3: The near background shows detailed foliage, bushes, and small rocks, adding texture and complexity.
