Background for 2d side-scrolling game, which have 4 separate horizontal layers. Layer 1: a distant mountain range with subtle gradients and silhouettes, using dark blues and muted greens to create depth. Layer 2: mid-distant rolling hills and sparse trees, with lighter greens and blues, adding some variation with small clusters of foliage. Layer 3: near background with detailed trees, bushes, and rocky formations, incorporating brighter blues and earthy tones for contrast.
