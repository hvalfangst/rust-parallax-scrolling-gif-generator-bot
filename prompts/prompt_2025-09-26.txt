Background for 2d side-scrolling game, which have 4 separate horizontal layers. Layer 1: (Far background, distant sky and clouds) features a gradient sky with subtle cloud shapes, using light blues and soft whites, with some distant mountain silhouettes in dark blue to add depth. Layer 2: (Mid-distant elements, hills and trees) includes rolling hills and sparse trees in muted greens and blues, with some gentle variation to suggest distance.
