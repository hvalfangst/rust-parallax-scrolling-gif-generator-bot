Background for 2d side-scrolling game, which have 4 separate horizontal layers. Layer 2: The mid-distant layer includes rolling hills and sparse trees, with soft shading to create a sense of atmospheric perspective. Layer 3: The near background contains detailed trees, bushes, and rocky formations, with more defined shapes and shadows.
