Background for 2d side-scrolling game, which have 4 separate horizontal layers. Layer 1: a distant mountain range with subtle peaks in dark blue and teal shades, blending into the sky; Layer 2: mid-distant rolling hills with gentle curves in muted greens and browns; Layer 3: near background trees and bushes with detailed foliage in earthy tones; Layer 4: foreground elements like grass, rocks, and small plants with vibrant colors and more detail.
