Background for 2d side-scrolling game, which have 4 separate horizontal layers. Layer 1: The far background features a distant mountain range with layered peaks in muted blues and grays, creating depth and atmospheric perspective. Layer 2: The mid-distant layer depicts rolling hills and sparse trees in softer greens and browns, adding a sense of scale. Layer 3: Near background includes detailed bushes and small trees with varied foliage in richer greens and earthy tones, providing texture.
